About the game

Alchemist 404 is an experimental roguelike deckbuilder inspired by real-world chemistry.

Isekai'd into a fantasy world, you fight elemental dragons with a deck built around sword mastery and become the hero you were never supposed to be.

1 / 2

Features

  • Tag-Based Cards

    Cards are defined by tags and effects instead of fixed damage numbers, so your hero's stats determine how powerful they become.

  • Slot-Based Combat

    Place cards into different slots to predict, counter, and outplay your enemy's actions.

  • Simultaneous Turns

    Plan your moves, commit your cards, then reveal the enemy's hidden actions and watch both sides resolve at once.

  • Alchemy & Reactions

    Combine elements to trigger reactions inspired by real-world chemistry.
